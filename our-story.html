OUR STORY

Made by hand. Rooted in nature.

Wood Vida began with a simple belief — that beautiful furniture should feel connected to the material it comes from.

THE BEGINNING

A love for wood, a respect for craft.

Wood Vida started offline with a passion for creating pieces that carry the character of natural wood.

Every piece is approached as something individual — not simply another object to fill a room, but something that can become part of a home for years to come.

Anay Kumar Founder, Wood Vida
OUR PHILOSOPHY

We don't try to hide nature.

Grain, texture, imperfections and variation are part of what makes natural wood beautiful. We preserve those details instead of covering them up.

THE PROCESS

to character.
