OUR PHILOSOPHY

Not Just Art.
Nature Preserved.

We believe beautiful wood does not need to come from cutting down a living tree. The character we value already exists in nature.

THE IDEA

We don't create
nature's character.
We preserve it.

Every grain, curve, crack and natural mark tells a story. Instead of covering that story, WOOD VIDA builds around it.

OUR MATERIAL

Wood With A Past

01

Naturally Fallen

Wood that has already fallen naturally becomes the beginning of a new object.

02

Broken & Aged

Time, weather and age create textures and forms that cannot be manufactured.

03

Discarded Wood

Pieces that might otherwise be forgotten can be given a new purpose.

04

Unique Character

No two pieces carry exactly the same grain, shape or natural history.

OUR RESPONSIBILITY

What We
Don't Do.

01

No unnecessary cutting

We do not build our philosophy around harvesting living trees simply for their wood.

02

No identical repetition

Natural materials are not forced into identical shapes just for uniformity.

03

No hiding imperfections

Cracks, grain and natural marks are part of the identity of each piece.

PRESERVATION

Protecting what
nature created.

01

Clean

The wood is carefully cleaned while respecting its existing character.

02

Stabilize

Fragile areas are prepared and stabilized so the piece can have a longer life.

03

Shape

Craftsmanship adds function without erasing the original form.

04

Finish

The final finish protects the material while keeping its natural visual identity.

THE WOOD VIDA BELIEF

Nature does not
make duplicates.

That's why every WOOD VIDA creation is inherently one of a kind.
