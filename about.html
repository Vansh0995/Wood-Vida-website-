ABOUT WOOD VIDA

Not Just Art.
Nature Preserved.

WOOD VIDA exists to give beautiful wood another life — transforming naturally fallen, broken, aged and discarded wood into meaningful pieces for modern spaces.

OUR BELIEF

Beautiful wood
does not need
to begin with a tree
being cut down.

We believe nature has already created incredible forms, textures, cracks, grains and silhouettes. Our role is not to replace them, but to preserve them.

WHAT WE CREATE

Objects With A Story

01

Side Lamps

Warm lighting combined with naturally formed wood.

02

Wall Art

Organic shapes that bring the texture of nature indoors.

03

Tables

Functional furniture built around the character of the original wood.

OUR APPROACH

From Forgotten
To Remarkable.

01

Discover

We search for naturally fallen, broken, aged and discarded wood with unique character.

02

Select

Every piece is carefully chosen for its texture, structure and story.

03

Preserve

We clean, stabilize and protect the natural character of the wood.

04

Create

Patient craftsmanship transforms the piece into art or furniture.

THE WOOD VIDA PHILOSOPHY

Nature already
made the art.

Every crack, grain, curve and mark is part of a story that existed long before the piece reached our workshop.

We do not believe in making every piece look identical. We believe in preserving the individuality that makes natural wood extraordinary.
